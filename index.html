⚡ Instant Polity Points

  • The Constitution is the supreme law of India.
  • Fundamental Rights protect important freedoms and interests of citizens.
  • Directive Principles provide guidelines for governance and social justice.
  • The Indian Parliament consists of the President, Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha.
  • The Supreme Court is the highest court of India.
  • Federalism involves division of powers between different levels of government.
  • Democracy is based on participation, representation and accountability.
  • The Election Commission conducts and supervises elections according to constitutional provisions.
